The room

The room does half the work.

Our address used to be a film studio. We kept the bones — 8,400 square feet of poured concrete and north light — and rebuilt the rest. Twelve meters of turf, Eleiko bars on every platform, a plunge that holds 4°C, and a sauna with room for six, not sixty.

Nothing here is decorative. Everything is load-bearing.
