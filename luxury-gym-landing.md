## Audience

- High-net-worth professionals, executives, and public figures who value privacy, exclusivity, and results.
- Time-poor members who want personalized coaching, not crowded classes.
- Skeptical first-time visitors who need proof (the room, the coaches, member outcomes) before they'll ever contact us.

## Core offer

- Members-only training club with a hard cap on membership.
- Strength & performance, recovery & longevity, and nutrition — coached individually, not taught in groups of fifty.
- The promise, in one line: **a smaller room and a higher standard.**

## Voice — guardrails (this page must not read as AI-generated)

Rules applied to every line of copy below:

- **State, don't sell.** No "welcome to", "we believe", "at NOIR we", "state-of-the-art", "world-class", "unleash", "empower", "seamless", "elevate", "curated", "journey", "unlock".
- **No exclamation marks** in body copy.
- **Numbers and constraints instead of adjectives.** Square footage, Celsius, member caps, years on the platform — not "premium", "exclusive", "unmatched".
- **Short declarative sentences.** Each one carries weight alone.
- **No stacked trios** of adjectives or features. One idea per sentence.
- Privacy and precision are the recurring themes; the room does the talking.

## CTA logic

- One primary action — **book a tour**. Solid gold button appears in the hero (once per viewport) and once at the end of the form section. Everything else is ghost/text: "Request a tour" (nav), "Explore programs", "Meet the coaches", tier text links.
- The nav "Request a tour" and the form anchor are distinct copy, never word-for-word duplicates of the primary.

## SEO plan

- **Title:** `NOIR — Private Fitness Club | Luxury Gym & Personal Training in [Neighborhood]`
- **Meta description:** "Members-only luxury gym in [Neighborhood], [City]. Private strength coaching, a recovery suite, and nutrition written for your week — capped membership, no crowds. Book a tour."
- **Domain / canonical:** `[noirclub.com]` placeholder `[verify]`
- **Natural keywords woven into copy (not stuffed):** private fitness club, luxury gym `[city]`, personal training `[neighborhood]`, strength coaching, recovery, nutrition. City and neighborhood appear once in the hero eyebrow, once in the footer, and in meta/title.
- **Structure:** exactly one H1 (hero). H2 per section, H3 for program/coach/tier cards. Logical order mirrors the table above.
- **Images:** descriptive alt text on every generated image (e.g., "Strength floor at NOIR private fitness club, [City]").
- **Schema:** LocalBusiness (Gym) JSON-LD — name, address, geo, openingHours, telephone, sameAs — plus FAQPage if we add the optional FAQ (see open notes).
- **Metadata:** canonical link, og:title/description/image, theme-color.
- **Craft marks:** clean semantic HTML, no horizontal scroll on mobile, lazy-loaded images below the fold.

## Media needs

- Generate realistic imagery via media tools (`media generate --surface image`): moody dark gym interiors (open concrete, dramatic light), a focused training moment, a recovery suite (plunge/sauna), three coach portraits, and one wide hero shot. Photorealistic, warm-light, black-and-gold grade. All media copied into `assets/`, referenced relatively.
- Optional FAQ (small, SEO-driven): 3–4 questions — "Do I need to be a member to book a tour?" · "Is the coaching included or separate?" · "How long does a membership run?" · "Do you accept guests?"

## Responsive & interaction

- Mobile: stacked layout, no horizontal scroll, nav collapses to a menu, touch targets ≥ 44px.
- Hover/focus pairs keep contrast: gold buttons deepen via oklch L shift; text never lightens toward background. Clear `:focus-visible` ring on every focusable element.
- Motion 150–250ms, keyed to accent; restrained reveals only where they add craft.

## Visual system notes

- Bind Luxury tokens verbatim into `:root` (bg `#080706`, surface `#151310`, accent `#c6a15b`, fg `#fff8ea`, border `#3a3020`, Didot/Avenir stacks).

- Didot oversized display for headlines, Avenir Next for body, tracked caps for eyebrows.
- No purple gradients, no emoji-as-icons, no hand-drawn figures, no warm-beige default.
